# AI Resume Builder

### Build Your Career. One Resume at a Time.

An AI-powered platform designed to simplify resume creation, improve professional profiles, and help individuals present their skills and experience effectively.

We're building a smarter way to create professional, ATS-friendly resumes — combining AI-powered assistance, intelligent content suggestions, and modern resume design into one seamless experience.

---

## ✨ About

Creating a professional resume shouldn't be complicated.

Many students and job seekers struggle to present their experience, communicate their skills, and create resumes that meet modern recruitment standards.

Our platform aims to solve this through AI-powered resume creation and optimization.

Whether you're a university student preparing for your first internship or an experienced professional exploring new opportunities, the platform helps you create resumes that communicate your value.

## 🚀 Key Features

### AI-Powered Resume Generation
- Generate professional resume content using AI.
- Turn basic information into impactful descriptions.
- Improve work experience and project descriptions.
- Generate professional summaries and career objectives.

### ATS-Friendly Resumes
- Create resumes structured for Applicant Tracking Systems.
- Optimize keywords based on job descriptions.
- Improve readability and content organization.
- Identify missing skills and relevant information.

### Professional Resume Builder
- Modern, customizable resume templates.
- Live resume preview.
- Structured sections for education, experience, skills and projects.
- Export resumes into professional PDF documents.

### AI Resume Optimization
- Analyze existing resumes.
- Get suggestions for improving content.
- Identify weak descriptions and missing information.
- Tailor resumes to specific job opportunities.

### Career Assistance
- Skills and experience presentation.
- Project description generation.
- Professional summary assistance.
- Job-specific resume recommendations.

## 🎯 Our Mission

We believe every individual deserves access to professional career-building tools.

Our mission is to make professional resume creation accessible, intelligent, and effortless — especially for students and early-career professionals.

This platform originated as a project within **InvertisPrep**, an educational platform built to support students at Invertis University.

As the idea evolved, we decided to develop it into an independent product with its own identity, infrastructure, and roadmap.

Our vision extends beyond university students — making intelligent career-building tools accessible to everyone.
